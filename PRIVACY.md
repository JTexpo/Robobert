# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Robobert ("Robobert", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use or interact with the Robobert Discord bot.

Robobert operates through Discord and may process information made available to it through Discord's platform and through commands you explicitly use.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on how Robobert is configured and which commands you use, the Bot may process:

### Discord Information

This may include:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server/guild IDs
* Discord channel IDs
* Discord role IDs
* Discord usernames, display names, or other publicly available Discord profile information
* Information contained in messages or command interactions that are explicitly provided to the Bot

Robobert does not need to collect every category of information listed above for every interaction. The information processed depends on the command being used and the permissions granted to the Bot.

### Usage Information

The Bot may process information necessary to operate its features, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability.

This can include command usage, error information, timestamps, and technical information associated with an interaction.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Robobert may be used to:

* Provide requested bot functionality.
* Respond to Discord commands and interactions.
* Maintain and improve the Bot.
* Diagnose errors and technical problems.
* Prevent abuse, fraud, or malicious activity.
* Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
* Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Data Retention

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Some information may be retained longer when necessary for security, abuse prevention, debugging, legal compliance, or other legitimate operational purposes.

Where information is no longer reasonably necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Robobert relies on Discord to provide its core functionality.

Your interactions with Discord are governed by Discord's own terms and privacy practices.

Robobert may also interact with other third-party services where necessary to provide particular functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

## 5. Discord Data

Robobert is designed to use Discord information only as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality.

Robobert does not intentionally request or collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card numbers, or authentication credentials.

You should never provide passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, or other secrets to the Bot.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by Robobert.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You acknowledge that information transmitted over the internet may carry inherent security risks.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Robobert is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that Robobert has collected information that should not have been collected, please contact the project maintainer so that the matter can be reviewed.

## 8. Your Choices

You may stop using Robobert at any time by removing the Bot from your Discord server or ceasing to interact with it.

Depending on the nature of the information and applicable law, you may also request information about personal data associated with your use of the Bot or request its deletion.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification requirements.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be published in this repository with a revised "Last updated" date.

Your continued use of Robobert after an updated Privacy Policy becomes available constitutes acceptance of the updated policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please open an issue in the Robobert repository or contact the project maintainer through the project's official contact method.
